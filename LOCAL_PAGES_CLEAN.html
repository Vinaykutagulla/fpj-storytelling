Every phase of drug development is where you'll work

Phase 01
Discovery & Research
⏱ 3–6 years | ~10,000 compounds screened | Only 250 advance
💊 The Drug

Target ID → Lead Selection

Scientists identify disease targets and screen thousands of compounds. Most fail—that's intentional. Only the best survive.

Target identification & validation
High-throughput compound screening
Molecular structure optimisation
Lead candidate selection
🎓 Your Role & Training

Foundation & Science Literacy

Build pharmaceutical vocabulary, understand drug targets and mechanism of action, work through real discovery case studies.

Research Scientist Medicinal Chemist Lab Technician Bioinformatician
🧪
Phase 02
Preclinical Testing
⏱ 2–4 years | In vitro & in vivo | IND application filed at end
💊 The Drug

Safety Before Humans

Exhaustive toxicology and pharmacokinetics in lab models and animals. One failed safety signal kills the compound—protecting patients.

In vitro & in vivo toxicity testing
ADME: absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion
Genotoxicity & carcinogenicity studies
IND / CTA dossier preparation
🎓 Your Role & Training

Safety Science & Data Skills

Master toxicology, ADME concepts, and data analysis workflows. Prove competency through structured assessments before advancing.

Preclinical Scientist Toxicologist DMPK Specialist Data Analyst
👥
Phase 03
Clinical Trials — Phase I
⏱ 1–2 years | 20–100 healthy volunteers | Safety & dosage testing
💊 The Drug

First-in-Human: Everything Watched

Most critical milestone. Healthy adults take the drug for the first time. Every parameter—every side effect—is recorded and reported rigorously.

Safety & tolerability in healthy volunteers
Dose escalation to find maximum tolerated dose
Adverse event monitoring per GCP standards
Pharmacokinetic sampling & analysis
🎓 Your Role & Training

GCP & Clinical Monitoring

Learn Good Clinical Practice end-to-end. Practice clinical site monitoring and master adverse event reporting—skills for your first pharma job.

CRA Medical Monitor Study Coordinator PV Officer
📊
Phase 04
Clinical Trials — Phase II & III
⏱ 2–5 years | 100 to 3,000+ patients | Efficacy proven globally
💊 The Drug

Does It Work? Does It Work Better?

Phase II: Does it work in patients? Phase III: Does it work better than existing treatments in thousands across multiple countries simultaneously?

Multi-site randomised controlled trials
Efficacy endpoints & comparative effectiveness
SUSAR reporting & safety data management
CDISC standards, EDC, & biostatistics
🎓 Your Role & Training

Data Management & Medical Writing

Master clinical data management tools, CDISC datasets, and medical writing for CSRs and regulatory submissions—the backbone of trial teams.

Clinical Data Manager Medical Writer Biostatistician CRA
📋
Phase 05
Regulatory Review & Approval
⏱ 1–2 years | FDA / EMA / CDSCO | Market authorisation granted
💊 The Drug

The Dossier That Decides Everything

Comprehensive CTD-format submission. Regulators scrutinise every study, dataset, and claim. One incomplete section delays approval by years.

NDA / MAA dossier compilation (CTD format)
Regulatory agency query responses
Risk management plans (EU-RMP, REMS)
SmPC, labelling, patient leaflet finalisation
🎓 Your Role & Training

Regulatory Affairs & Certification

Build your professional portfolio of completed projects. Earn certifications proving industry-readiness to regulatory and compliance employers.

RA Specialist Compliance Officer RA Manager
🚀
Phase 06
Manufacturing & Market Launch
⏱ Ongoing | Millions of doses | Post-market surveillance never stops
💊 The Drug

Approval ≠ Done. It Must Be Perfect.

Drug must be manufactured at scale with zero compromise, distributed worldwide, and monitored indefinitely for real-world safety signals.

GMP-compliant commercial manufacturing
Quality control, batch release, stability testing
Post-market surveillance & Phase IV studies
Medical information & pharmacovigilance
