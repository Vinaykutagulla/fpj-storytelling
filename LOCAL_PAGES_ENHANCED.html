6 Phases · Drug Development + Your Career

Every phase of drug development
is a career opportunity

Left side: what happens to the drug. Right side: what you learn and where you work. One story, told together.

🔬
Phase 01
Discovery & Research
⏱ 3–6 years · ~10,000 compounds screened · Only 250 advance
💊 The Drug

Target ID → Lead Selection

Scientists identify a disease target and screen thousands of compounds for any that interact with it. Most fail immediately — that's the design. Only the best leads survive.

Target identification & biological validation High-throughput compound screening Molecular structure optimisation Lead candidate selection
🎓 Your Training at FPJ

Foundation & Science Literacy

You build your pharmaceutical science vocabulary, understand drug targets and mechanism of action, and work through real discovery-phase case studies.

Research Scientist Medicinal Chemist Lab Technician Bioinformatician
🧪
Phase 02
Preclinical Testing
⏱ 2–4 years · In vitro & in vivo · IND application filed at end
💊 The Drug

Safety Before Any Human Takes It

Exhaustive toxicology and pharmacokinetics studies in lab models and animals. One failed safety signal here kills the compound — saving patients from harm down the line.

In vitro & in vivo toxicity testing ADME — absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion Genotoxicity, carcinogenicity studies IND / CTA dossier preparation
🎓 Your Training at FPJ

Safety Science & Data Skills

You master toxicology principles, ADME concepts, and data analysis workflows — then prove your understanding through structured assessments before progressing.

Preclinical Scientist Toxicologist DMPK Specialist Data Analyst
👥
Phase 03
Clinical Trials — Phase I
⏱ 1–2 years · 20–100 healthy volunteers · Safety & dosage only
💊 The Drug

First-in-Human: Everything Watched

The most critical milestone in drug development. A small group of healthy adults takes the drug for the first time. Every parameter — every side effect — is recorded and reported.

Safety & tolerability in healthy volunteers Dose escalation to find maximum tolerated dose Adverse event monitoring & reporting per GCP Pharmacokinetic sampling & analysis
🎓 Your Training at FPJ

GCP, Monitoring & AE Reporting

You learn Good Clinical Practice end-to-end, practice clinical site monitoring, and master adverse event reporting — skills you'll use from your very first pharma job.

CRA Medical Monitor Study Coordinator PV Officer
📊
Phase 04
Clinical Trials — Phase II & III
⏱ 2–5 years · 100 to 3,000+ patients · Efficacy proven at scale
💊 The Drug

Does It Work? Does It Work Better?

Phase II asks if the drug actually works in patients. Phase III proves it works better than what already exists — in thousands of people, across multiple countries, simultaneously.

Multi-site randomised controlled trials Efficacy endpoints & comparative effectiveness SUSAR reporting, safety data management CDISC standards, EDC, biostatistics
🎓 Your Training at FPJ

Data Management & Medical Writing

You master clinical data management tools, CDISC datasets, and medical writing for CSRs and regulatory submissions — the backbone of every large-scale trial team.

Clinical Data Manager Medical Writer Biostatistician CRA
📋
Phase 05
Regulatory Review & Approval
⏱ 1–2 years · FDA / EMA / CDSCO · Market authorisation granted
💊 The Drug

The Dossier That Decides Everything

All data compiled into a comprehensive CTD-format submission. Regulators scrutinise every study, every dataset, every claim. One incomplete section can delay approval by years.

NDA / MAA dossier compilation (CTD format) Responses to regulatory agency queries Risk management plans (EU-RMP, REMS) SmPC, labelling, patient leaflet finalisation
🎓 Your Training at FPJ

Regulatory Affairs & Certification

You build your professional portfolio — your own "dossier" of completed projects — and earn certifications that signal industry-readiness to regulatory and compliance employers.

Regulatory Affairs Specialist Compliance Officer RA Manager
🚀
Phase 06
Manufacturing & Market Launch
⏱ Ongoing · Millions of doses · Post-market surveillance never stops
💊 The Drug

Approved Is Not Enough — It Must Be Perfect

The drug must now be manufactured at scale with zero quality compromise, distributed to patients worldwide, and monitored indefinitely for any real-world safety signals.

GMP-compliant commercial manufacturing Quality control, batch release, stability testing Post-market surveillance & Phase IV studies Medical information & ongoing pharmacovigilance
